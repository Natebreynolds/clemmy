# v3.18.60 — Clem on your phone is one screen, and a chat follows you between desktop and phone

**One database migration.** The harness schema stays at 102. The memory
database moves to schema 38: one added index, so semantic recall walks older
notes in order. Once this build opens your memory, v3.18.59 refuses to open
it (it never downgrades a newer schema), so going back means restoring a
backup taken before the update. The Mac build comes from the release workflow
(signed and notarized). The Windows x64 build of the same commit is qualified
by the private Windows pipeline from this tag, unsigned until a code-signing
certificate exists.

Proven on the installed app: every change below was hotpatched onto the
owner's running Mac (r59c1 to r59c21) and driven live, including a first-time
owner's walk through the phone and a chat continued from desktop to phone.

## Clem on your phone is one screen

Opening the app shows Clem herself: her name and face (a ring breathes while
she works), one line of where things stand, what she is working on, the
conversation that waits on your answer, and what needs you. The five tabs, the
Chats pill and the More button are gone. Everything else (chats, Inbox,
Spaces, automations, work, memory, apps and devices) lives in **Your stuff**,
the button beside the composer, and every place opens with a way back to Clem.

- Clementine's own colours: the cream canvas, white cards, warm ink, and
  clementine for what needs you.
- A card leads with Clem's question; the answers that act are buttons, and
  Reply, Later and Dismiss are one quiet line.
- **Waiting on your answer**: a conversation where Clem asked you something
  leads her screen and says so in Chats.
- Automations read in words: On, On demand, Running, Needs you; schedules as
  "Weekdays · 9 AM".
- An approval for a calendar change reads "Fri, Oct 30 at 1:00 PM – 1:45 PM".
- On the Duo's front screen, everything stays clear of the system rail.

No new iPhone build is needed: the phone app comes from your Mac.

## A chat follows you between desktop and phone

A conversation started on the desktop and continued on the phone (or the other
way round) keeps its history. Before, the phone was treated as someone else
and Clem started over. Stop and Continue on the phone work on a desktop chat
too. Shared channels still keep each person's conversation separate.

## Runs you missed while Clementine was closed

Scheduled runs that came due while the app was closed are offered on Today
(desktop and phone), never fired by surprise on reopen: tick the ones you
want, run them now or skip them, and watch them go.

## The desktop

Codex's refinements to Automations (a new editor and recovery), Today's first
use, the Spaces gallery, Goals, Help (suggestions open as drafts you can
edit), and the Settings cards. First run gains an optional **Phone** step with
the pairing code, and Apps & devices reads in plain words and shows your apps
right after a restart.

## Also

- Deleting a workflow or archiving a chat clears its notices from Needs you.
- Sending to the one account you have connected no longer turns into a
  question about which account.
- Recall reaches older vault notes; project knowledge directories and briefs.
- A new workflow's "today" means today: the workflow skill teaches date tokens.
- A creation test reads each step's real result, so setting up a workflow takes
  about half the time and tokens.
- Clem asks about what you want, never about what one of her guides wants.
- Quick voice actions and a paired Watch conversation (iPhone build 21).
