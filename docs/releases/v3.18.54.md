# v3.18.54 — Clem on the iPhone Duo and a tab bar on every phone, faster Spaces, and fewer dead ends when Clem uses her own tools

**No database migration** (harness schema stays at 99). The Mac build comes
from the release workflow (signed and notarized). The Windows x64 build of
the same commit is qualified by the private Windows pipeline from this tag,
**unsigned** until a code-signing certificate exists.

## On your phone

- **Tabs along the bottom.** Today, Chats, Needs you (with its count) and
  Spaces sit in a tab bar, and More opens the menu with everything else.
  The tabs step aside while you type and inside a chat.
- **iPhone Duo.** Opened up, Clem reads as two pages: the menu and recent
  chats on the left, the screen you are on at the right, with nothing on
  the fold. On the small front screen the tabs stand in the strip beside
  the clock, like the home screen's dock, so the page and the message box
  get the bottom back.
- **Screens that are empty say what to do next.** Spaces, Automations,
  Agents and Projects each offer one button (ask Clem to build one, or
  start a new one), and the words match across screens.
- **Memory fits the phone.** Saved memories come first, filters use the
  same names as on your Mac, and activity details are one tap away.
- The keyboard no longer shows a second bar of arrows above Clem's message
  box (iPhone app build 18 on TestFlight).

## Speed

- **Spaces lists open in a fraction of the time.** Your Spaces list on the
  phone took about a second and a half on a hundred Spaces; it now takes
  about 50 ms, with the same results.
- Clem's background loop no longer re-reads every Space every 15 seconds
  because of a few old failing sources, which was the largest cause of the
  app pausing.

## Clem's answers

- **"Which workflows ran today?" is one step.** Clem can list every run in
  a time window, one line per workflow, instead of re-checking until she
  gave up.
- **"Which Spaces are failing?" is one step.** Her Spaces list now says
  what needs attention on each Space.
- **Saving a memory is quicker and plainer.** Clem no longer reads a fact
  back after saving it, does not ask where to file it, and tells you what
  she remembered without fact numbers or timestamps.
- When Clem calls one of her own tools the wrong way, she is told the right
  way to call it instead of being sent to make a plan, so a simple request
  no longer ends with "I wasn't able to".
- A memory change Clem's check could not pin down is no longer marked done
  by a partial save, and the readings behind "what is running" and safe
  updates now finish instead of reporting "could not verify".

## Known gaps

- Correcting a fact while Clem is still saving it (before the first save
  finished) can end with "I could not confirm that your correction replaced
  what I had saved before", although the new value is saved.
- An approval you give after Clem restarted can wait until the next
  restart before it runs.
- Before an update, Clem may list old workflow runs as active work; they
  are history from earlier runs.
- Duplicate replies were reported on the phone and are being looked into.
- Rehearsal and cancelled workflow runs can leave "Approve" cards in Needs
  you that do nothing when tapped.
