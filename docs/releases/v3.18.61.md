# v3.18.61 — Clem's checks run after you approve, keys go in a hidden field, and a computer task repeats without AI

**No database migration.** The harness schema stays at 102 and the memory
database at 38, so going back to v3.18.60 needs no backup. The Mac build comes
from the release workflow (signed and notarized). The Windows x64 build of the
same commit is qualified by the private Windows pipeline from this tag,
unsigned until a code-signing certificate exists.

Proven on the installed app: every change below was hotpatched onto the
owner's running Mac (r60c8 to r60c41) and driven live with test emails to the
owner, test agents and test folders that were deleted afterwards.

## After you approve, Clem's answer is still checked

When you tap Approve, Clem carries on in the same request, and the quality
check on her final answer now runs as that request. Before, every answer that
followed a tapped approval skipped its check. If the work stalls after you
approve, Clem tells you what happened in her own words.

One action asks once: an action Clem prepared twice (once queued, once as a
direct call) gets one card, while a different file, account, operation or
capability still gets its own. A review group in the Inbox lists the actions
it covers.

## A key goes into a hidden field, never the chat

When a connected tool is missing its key, Clem asks in her own words and her
question brings a card with a hidden field. The key is saved straight into
Clementine's settings; it never appears in the conversation, and you can
remove it the same way. On the phone, Clem asks you to enter it on the Mac.

## A computer task you did once repeats without AI

Under a reply where Clem used your computer, **Save to repeat without AI**
keeps the task as steps. Automations → Computer tasks repeats it: each step
finds its window and element again, with no model calls, and stops for Clem
if something moved.

## See which agent did the work

When Clem hands work to one of your saved agents, the chat names it ("Site
Scout: example.com"), shows its model in words and links to the agent's page.
The agent's page lists the work Clem handed it, with the chat each task came
from, instead of opening on an empty list.

## Plain words

- Approval cards read as the question Clem asked, before and after you
  decide ("You approved", "You declined"), never an operation name.
- A reply whose card you answered stops saying it is waiting.
- An out-of-credit card names only workflows that still exist and the
  service that refused.
- A card you dismissed in Needs you stays put away.

## Faster and leaner

- A run resumed by an approval sends its history compacted (one measured
  resume went from 258k tokens to 59k).
- An xAI brain keeps its cached prefix warm across a conversation.
- The quality check reads a summary of how Clem found her tools, not the
  instructions she needed for her next call.

## The desktop and phone

Codex's refinements to workflows and app connections on desktop and phone;
the computer control is one small button you can hide, and its view opens as
a side panel while Clem uses the computer.

## Also

- When more than one thing fits, Clem asks which one, keeps your answer, and
  offers to remember a way she had to find.
- An answer to Clem's question reaches memory with the question it answered;
  a fact you forgot stays forgotten; memory never keeps a credential.
- The Workflow Doctor moves to your backup model like the steps do.
- Clem's voice never falls back to the device's voice, and says what is
  wrong with the key.
