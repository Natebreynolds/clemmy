# v3.18.25 — Clem notices, any calendar, cheaper write reviews, no dead ends

Mac-only release. No schema change (88). Qualification and live evidence
below are recorded as measured; signed and notarized artifacts come from the
release workflow and are not claimed here.

## What changed

### Clem notices, and asks

- A new heartbeat, **Noticing**, looks across your goals and what is going on
  — runs, waits, drafts, the calendar, recent conversations, what she
  remembers — and proposes at most one thing worth doing, with the evidence
  it rests on. It arrives as a question in Needs You. Answer in your own
  words: "do it" starts the work as an ordinary chat turn (every approval
  rule unchanged), "not now" sets it aside, "never …" is remembered and
  honoured. How often she thinks and how many proposals a day are settings.
- **Your goals** now lead the Goals page: status, priority, progress notes,
  next actions and blockers, editable in place, with what Clem proposed for
  each goal and your answers.
- **Clem thinking**: under the Noticing heartbeat, each round's reading, what
  she considered and why she proposed or set it aside.

### Any calendar

- The calendar watch no longer names two providers. For each connected app it
  learns the app's own calendar read once: one short call over every
  connected app's whole operation list, by id only, that ranks up to three
  candidate reads per app; then the candidates' own definitions, one at a
  time, until one yields a recipe that passes its checks. Apps with no
  calendar are remembered so they are not asked again.
- When learning fails, the watch says why (and when it will try again),
  instead of "no calendar connected".
- A provider changing its calendar operation no longer breaks the watch: each
  read is prepared against the provider's current definition first. (On the
  owner's machine 443 of 768 reads had failed this way since 09-25.)

### Cheaper workflow write reviews

- A proposed write in a workflow step is reviewed at the depth its stakes
  need: an ordinary recoverable write gets a measured review first and the
  full review only on a doubt; a send, a delete or an irreversible change
  gets the full review from the start.
- The review reads a compact index of the run's evidence, leads with what a
  step keeps the same (so repeated reviews reuse the provider's cache), and
  says where each proposed value already appears in what the run read.
- A write held because another attempt of the same step took over is told
  so; it is no longer sent to "repair its arguments".

### No dead ends

- Approving the card for a write to a credential file (`.env` and the like)
  now lands the write once; before, the approved call was refused and a
  second card opened.
- The phone has the **Approvals** setting: Auto or Ask, the same switch as the
  Mac, with what Ask mode has learned and a Forget for each.

### Under the hood

- Every structured answer Clem asks a model for converts to the provider's
  strict schema form (pinned), and the evidence binding is the host's, not a
  value the model must copy back.

## Known limits

- The phone does not yet show Clem thinking or the goals page; proposals reach
  the phone as Needs You questions (push only where phone notifications are
  set up).
- The Heartbeats page lists the four built-in heartbeats for everyone; the
  calendar card shows even with no calendar connected. Watches that Clem
  proposes and you accept are the next step.
- A workflow step can still start a second attempt while the first is under
  review (seen once on 09-30); the held write now says so honestly, but the
  cause of the second attempt is open.
- Journey `ordinary-conversation-competitive-acceptance` (greeting-3) fails on
  3.18.24 too; inherited.
- A complete answer that ends on an offer ("want me to…?") can be closed as a
  question waiting on you when the fast checker scores it just over its bar.
  Inherited; next wave.
- The chat model occasionally calls a tool before searching for it; the host
  refuses and it recovers in an extra round or two. Inherited.
- Under heavy machine load the app's watchdog timer can hold the main loop
  for 15–55 seconds. Inherited; next wave.
- Review cost on a scheduled workflow run (platform-49) is measured after
  this tag, against 3.18.24's 23 reviewer rounds, 550k prompt tokens, 368 s.
