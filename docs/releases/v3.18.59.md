# v3.18.59 — Clem shows her work on the card you answered, and other apps can hand her a task

**No database migration.** The harness schema stays at 102, so this build
opens a v3.18.58 database as it is, and a Mac on this build can still go back
to v3.18.58. The Mac build comes from the release workflow (signed and
notarized). The Windows x64 build of the same commit is qualified by the
private Windows pipeline from this tag, unsigned until a code-signing
certificate exists.

Proven on the installed app: every change below was hotpatched onto the
owner's running Mac (r58c8 to r58c11) and driven live with named fixtures and
the owner's own card replies.

## A new look, everywhere

Codex's redesign of Today, Inbox, Work, Spaces and Automations: one Needs you,
set-aside questions with Undo, app guidance, task results, and the computer
dock. Same data, plainer words, fewer screens to learn.

## The card you answered keeps the record

Reply on one of Clem's items, by tapping a choice or in your own words, and
that item now shows what she is doing about it, on desktop and phone:

- **You:** your words, then each real action she takes on your apps in plain
  words as it happens ("Outlook get calendar view ✓"). Her own housekeeping
  (looking up tools, recalling memory) is not listed.
- **Her answer**, or what she still needs: **Clem needs your OK** with
  **Review and approve**, which opens the approval card itself with the
  exact change.
- The record outlives a reload; once the item leaves the list it stays under
  Just now.

Words typed on an item now always reach her. Before, a question still open in
her thread could hold them, and the reply ended unseen.

## Clem is more proactive, and says less noise

- **One card per cause.** Workflows stopped by the same account problem (out
  of credit, usage used up) are one card naming each of them, not one card per
  failed run; the card leaves once they all run cleanly.
- **A go-ahead that never started, starts.** If the app stopped before an
  approved suggestion began, it starts once more in the same chat instead of
  waiting forever as "accepted".
- **A workflow step can ask you.** The run waits in Inbox for your answer and
  carries on with it.
- **Switch, then tell, in plain names.** A step that ran on your backup model
  says which and why ("ran on Grok 4.7 because the account behind DeepSeek
  V4.1 Flash is out of credit").

## Other apps can hand Clem a task

Connect gains **Let other apps hand Clem work**: one tap adds Clem to Claude
Desktop or Cursor (only her entry; the app's other settings stay, and a copy
of the original is kept). From there, ask Clem for something; she does it here
with her memory and tools, and anything that needs you waits for you here.
Nothing in another app can approve for you.

## One browser on Mac and Linux

Your computer is Clem's browser. The cloud browser and the Chrome harness are
retired on macOS and Linux (Windows keeps them until the computer driver is
proven there); until your computer is on, Clem offers that one tap instead.

## Approval cards in plain lines

An approval reads as lines, never as JSON: a message body shows its text, and
ids stay under Technical details.

## Also

- The computer driver stops with the app, and one an earlier session left
  behind is stopped at the next start.
- A send through the screen asks like a send through a connected app.
- Acting on a web page through your computer asks once and teaches.
- Clem opens a browser of her own for pages that need no sign-in, and never
  takes over a tab you had open.
- Sharing a plugin refuses embedded MCP credentials; a held turn is never
  reported as answered by `clementine run`; plural memory commands go to the
  ordinary memory tools.
