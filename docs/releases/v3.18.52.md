# v3.18.52 — tapping a choice outside a chat runs it, and an approved suggestion starts its work

**No database migration** (harness schema stays at 99). The Mac build comes
from the release workflow (signed and notarized). The Windows x64 build of
the same commit is qualified by the private Windows pipeline from this tag,
**unsigned** until a code-signing certificate exists.

## What you tap on Home or in a notification runs

- **A tapped choice is not held by an earlier question.** Every "Accept",
  "Decline" or "Run it again" you tap on Home or in a notification runs in
  Clem's own thread. When a question had been left open in that thread (one
  you never saw from where you tapped, such as "which mailbox should I
  use?"), every later tap was refused in under a second with "I could not
  safely load the current capability catalog for this request. Nothing was
  started. Please retry." A tapped choice approves exactly that action, so
  an open question elsewhere in the thread no longer holds it.
- **An approved suggestion starts its work.** Tapping "do it" on one of
  Clem's suggestions accepted your answer but never started the task, and
  about ten minutes later the app closed the conversation with "A message
  was accepted but never answered". The task now starts when you approve
  it.

## Known gap

After a tap, Home still says only "Started a conversation." The result, or
a question Clem asks about it, appears in her chat rather than on the item
you tapped. Showing the outcome where you tapped is the next change.
