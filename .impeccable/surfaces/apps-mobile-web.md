# Clem on the phone

Scope: apps/mobile-web (the phone app and the iOS shell's web content). Mode: Operate.
Audience: a busy owner, often a first-time AI user, glancing between meetings or late at night, one thumb.
Job: see what needs them, answer on the card, ask Clem in plain words, find everything else in one place.
Constraints: 100% light (owner directive 08-25); Clementine brand colours only (owner 10-30); works on the Duo
front screen beside a system rail; external writes always open the exact-content approval card.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Clem is the screen — her name, her face, one sentence of where things stand, what she is doing, what waits on you, and the composer. Refuses the five-tab dashboard and the stack of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Apple's own apps played straight in Clementine's colours: large titles, inset grouped white cards with one warm hairline on the cream canvas #faf7f2, warm ink, the system face, clementine #f26419 as fill with the ink label and #b94300 for text, spent only on what needs you and on Clem being alive.

STORY: the owner opens the app, reads one line of where things stand, answers what needs them on the card, asks Clem in the composer, and finds Spaces, automations and chats in Your stuff.

FIRST VIEWPORT: large "Clem" left, dog avatar right (a ring breathes while she works); greeting, then the headline ("17 things need you" in ink orange); an always-on status line; the conversation waiting on you first; Needs you cards led by Clem's question with the answers as buttons; floating composer with the round Your stuff button at its left.

FORM: the category standard (canon) chosen by the owner on the decision page over the roll's assigned direction ("The Pass") and the pick ("While You Were Out"); position 7 of 7 on the ordered list (the standing exit), raised to "next level" and recoloured to the brand. Seed key 3b599b77.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
