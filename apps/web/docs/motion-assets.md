- Warm terracotta studio and reddish-fawn Clem introduce the palette; deeper
  ink-brown environments create depth in the technical chapters. Amber light,
  cream highlights, soft shadows, and restrained coral accents connect them.
- One fine amber ribbon carries the story between scenes. It becomes a path,
  a completed lap, a retained memory, a tool connection, and a delegation lane.
  Its behavior explains relationships; avoid a generic field of network dots.
- Use matched entry and exit positions, consistent light from the upper left,
  and deliberate forward camera travel. No arbitrary zoom bursts or constant
  spinning. Keep a quiet region in every composition for readable HTML content.
- Generate character performance, atmosphere, tactile materials, and cinematic
  environments only. Render every label, tool name, model selection, diagram,
  code snippet, metric, and product interface in code. Generated media must not
  contain fabricated screenshots, unreadable text, or invented product claims.
- Use controlled illustrative tasks. The sequence explains framework behavior;
  it does not display or connect to the user's personal accounts or data.

geometry determines progress; no wheel interception or time-based scroll lock
is used. The idle film loops near the top. The handoff remains paused and seeks

manual handoff use the shared lazy media hook. Reduced motion or data saving

owned by the site's smoke/visual validation, separate from source-film acceptance.
